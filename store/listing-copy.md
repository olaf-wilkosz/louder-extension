## Detailed description

```
Louder turns any web page, email, or selected text into speech — with the words highlighted live as they're read, so your eyes and ears stay in sync.

FEATURES

📖 Read anything
Full pages, Gmail threads, or just a selection. Select 2+ words anywhere and a small "Read it louder!" trigger appears right where you're reading — click it and Louder starts immediately.

🎯 Word & sentence highlighting
Powered by the CSS Custom Highlight API — nothing is injected into the page, so your formatting stays untouched.

🗣️ Every voice your browser offers
Louder detects the language of what you're reading and picks a matching voice automatically, or choose your own from the full list.

⚡ Adjustable speed
From 0.25x for careful listening to 5x for a quick skim.

🎨 Highlight presets
Amber, Midnight, Forest, and Paper — pick whichever is easiest on your eyes.

🌓 Dark, Light, or System theme
Matches your OS automatically, or set it manually.

🔄 Cross-tab aware
Start reading in one tab and any other tab that was mid-sentence pauses automatically — no overlapping audio.

PRIVACY

Louder reads the page you're already looking at. It doesn't send your browsing data anywhere — all text-to-speech happens locally via your browser's built-in Web Speech API.
```
